Física · Compendio principal

Mecánica, fluidos, termodinámica, electricidad, magnetismo, ondas y óptica.

Vectores

$$\vec A=A_x\hat i+A_y\hat j+A_z\hat k$$ $$|\vec A|=\sqrt{A_x^2+A_y^2+A_z^2}$$ $$\hat u_A=\frac{\vec A}{|\vec A|}$$ $$\vec A\cdot\vec B=AB\cos\theta$$ $$|\vec A\times\vec B|=AB\sin\theta$$

Cinemática rectilínea

$$v_{\text{med}}=\frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t},\qquad a_{\text{med}}=\frac{\Delta v}{\Delta t}$$ $$v=\frac{dx}{dt},\qquad a=\frac{dv}{dt}=\frac{d^2x}{dt^2}$$

MRU

$$\Delta r=vt$$

MRUV

$$v_f=v_0+at$$ $$\Delta r=v_0t+\frac12at^2$$ $$v_f^2=v_0^2+2a\Delta r$$

Movimiento parabólico

$$v_{x}=v_0\cos\theta,\qquad v_{0y}=v_0\sin\theta$$ $$\Delta x=v_x\,t$$ $$\Delta y=v_{0y}\,t-\frac12gt^2$$ $$v_y=v_0\sin\theta-gt$$ $$t_v=\frac{2v_0\sin\theta}{g}$$ $$A=\frac{v_0^2\sin(2\theta)}{g}$$ $$h_{\max}=\frac{v_0^2\sin^2\theta}{2g}$$

Movimiento circular

$$\omega=\frac{d\theta}{dt},\qquad \alpha=\frac{d\omega}{dt}$$

MCU

$$\Delta \omega=\alpha t$$

MCUV

$$\omega_f=\omega_0+at$$ $$\Delta \theta=\omega_0t+\frac12at^2$$ $$v_f^2=v_0^2+2a\Delta \theta$$

Angular -> Lineal o tangencial

$$\Delta r=\Delta \theta \, r,\quad v=\omega \, r,\quad a=\alpha \, r$$ $$a_c=\frac{v^2}{r}=\omega^2r$$ $$ \vec{a_T}=\vec{a}+\vec{a_c}, \qquad a_T=\sqrt{a^2+a_c} $$ $$\omega=2\pi f,\qquad T=\frac1f$$

Dinámica

$$\sum\vec F=m\vec a$$ $$\vec P=m\vec g$$ $$F_s\le\mu_sN,\qquad F_k=\mu_kN$$ $$F_c=\frac{mv^2}{r}$$ $$\vec F_{AB}=-\vec F_{BA}$$

Trabajo

$$W=\int\vec F\cdot d\vec r$$ $$W=Fd\cos\theta$$

Energias

$$E_c=\frac12mv^2$$ $$E_p=mgh,\qquad E_k=\frac12kx^2$$ $$W_{\text{neto}}=\Delta E$$ $$E_i+W_{nc}=E_f$$

Potencia

$$P=\frac{dW}{dt}=\vec F\cdot\vec v$$

Cantidad de movimiento e impulso

$$\vec p=m\vec v$$ $$\vec J=\int\vec F\,dt=\Delta\vec p$$ $$\sum\vec p_i=\sum\vec p_f$$ $$e=\frac{v_{2f}-v_{1f}}{v_{1i}-v_{2i}}$$ $$\vec r_{cm}=\frac{\sum m_i\vec r_i}{\sum m_i}$$

Rotación y equilibrio

$$\vec\tau=\vec r\times\vec F$$ $$\sum\tau=I\alpha$$ $$E_{r }=\frac12I\omega^2$$ $$\vec L=I\vec\omega$$ $$\sum\vec F=0,\qquad \sum\tau=0$$ $$I=\int r^2\,dm$$

Gravitación

$$F=G\frac{m_1m_2}{r^2}$$ $$g=G\frac{M}{r^2}$$ $$U=-G\frac{Mm}{r}$$ $$v_{orb}=\sqrt{\frac{GM}{r}}$$ $$v_{esc}=\sqrt{\frac{2GM}{R}}$$ $$T^2=\frac{4\pi^2}{GM}r^3$$

Fluidos

$$\rho=\frac{m}{V},\qquad P=\frac{F}{A}$$ $$P=P_0+\rho gh$$ $$F_B=\rho_{fluido}gV_{desplazado}$$ $$A_1v_1=A_2v_2$$ $$P+\frac12\rho v^2+\rho gh=\text{constante}$$ $$Q=Av$$

Termodinámica

$$Q=mc\Delta T$$ $$Q=mL$$ $$PV=nRT$$ $$\Delta U=Q-W$$ $$W=\int_{V_i}^{V_f}P\,dV$$ $$\eta=\frac{W}{Q_H}=1-\frac{Q_C}{Q_H}$$ $$\eta_{Carnot}=1-\frac{T_C}{T_H}$$

Electrostática

$$F=k\frac{|q_1q_2|}{r^2}$$ $$\vec E=\frac{\vec F}{q_0}=k\frac{q}{r^2}\hat r$$ $$V=k\frac qr$$ $$U=qV=k\frac{q_1q_2}{r}$$ $$\Phi_E=\int\vec E\cdot d\vec A$$ $$\oint\vec E\cdot d\vec A=\frac{Q_{enc}}{\varepsilon_0}$$

Capacitancia y circuitos

$$C=\frac QV$$ $$C_{placas}=\varepsilon\frac Ad$$ $$U_C=\frac12CV^2=\frac{Q^2}{2C}$$ $$V=IR$$ $$P=VI=I^2R=\frac{V^2}{R}$$ $$R=\rho\frac LA$$ $$\sum I_{entra}=\sum I_{sale},\qquad \sum\Delta V=0$$

Resistencias y capacitores equivalentes

$$R_s=R_1+R_2+\cdots$$ $$\frac1{R_p}=\frac1{R_1}+\frac1{R_2}+\cdots$$ $$C_p=C_1+C_2+\cdots$$ $$\frac1{C_s}=\frac1{C_1}+\frac1{C_2}+\cdots$$ $$q_{carga}=CV\left(1-e^{-t/RC}\right)$$ $$q_{descarga}=Q_0e^{-t/RC}$$

Magnetismo e inducción

$$\vec F=q\vec v\times\vec B$$ $$\vec F=I\vec L\times\vec B$$ $$r=\frac{mv}{|q|B}$$ $$B_{hilo}=\frac{\mu_0I}{2\pi r}$$ $$\Phi_B=\int\vec B\cdot d\vec A$$ $$\mathcal{E}=-\frac{d\Phi_B}{dt}$$ $$\mathcal{E}_L=-L\frac{dI}{dt},\qquad U_L=\frac12LI^2$$

Ondas y sonido

$$v=\lambda f$$ $$y(x,t)=A\sin(kx-\omega t+\phi)$$ $$k=\frac{2\pi}{\lambda},\qquad \omega=2\pi f$$ $$v_{cuerda}=\sqrt{\frac{T}{\mu}}$$ $$I=\frac PA,\qquad \beta=10\log_{10}\left(\frac I{I_0}\right)$$ $$f'=f\frac{v\pm v_o}{v\mp v_s}$$

Óptica geométrica

$$n=\frac cv$$ $$n_1\sin\theta_1=n_2\sin\theta_2$$ $$\theta_i=\theta_r$$ $$\frac1f=\frac1{d_o}+\frac1{d_i}$$ $$m=\frac{h_i}{h_o}=-\frac{d_i}{d_o}$$ $$P_{lente}=\frac1f\quad(f\text{ en metros})$$

Relatividad y física moderna

$$E=mc^2$$ $$\gamma=\frac1{\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}}$$ $$\Delta t=\gamma\Delta t_0,\qquad L=\frac{L_0}{\gamma}$$ $$E=hf=\frac{hc}{\lambda}$$ $$\lambda_{de\ Broglie}=\frac hp$$ $$E_n=-\frac{13.6\ \text{eV}}{n^2}\qquad(\text{átomo de hidrógeno})$$
