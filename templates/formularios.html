Identidades trigonométricas

Relaciones fundamentales, círculo unitario y triángulos notables.

Identidades recíprocas y de cociente

$$\csc\theta=\frac{1}{\sin\theta},\qquad \sin\theta=\frac{1}{\csc\theta}$$ $$\sec\theta=\frac{1}{\cos\theta},\qquad \cos\theta=\frac{1}{\sec\theta}$$ $$\cot\theta=\frac{1}{\tan\theta},\qquad \tan\theta=\frac{1}{\cot\theta}$$ $$\tan\theta=\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta},\qquad \cot\theta=\frac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}$$

Identidades pitagóricas

$$\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1$$ $$1+\tan^2\theta=\sec^2\theta$$ $$1+\cot^2\theta=\csc^2\theta$$ $$\sin^2\theta=1-\cos^2\theta,\qquad \cos^2\theta=1-\sin^2\theta$$

Paridad, periodicidad y cofunciones

$$\sin(-\theta)=-\sin\theta,\quad \cos(-\theta)=\cos\theta,\quad \tan(-\theta)=-\tan\theta$$ $$\sin(\theta+2\pi)=\sin\theta,\quad \cos(\theta+2\pi)=\cos\theta$$ $$\tan(\theta+\pi)=\tan\theta$$ $$\sin\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-\theta\right)=\cos\theta,\quad \cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-\theta\right)=\sin\theta$$ $$\tan\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-\theta\right)=\cot\theta,\quad \sec\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-\theta\right)=\csc\theta$$

Suma y diferencia de ángulos

$$\sin(\alpha\pm\beta)=\sin\alpha\cos\beta\pm\cos\alpha\sin\beta$$ $$\cos(\alpha\pm\beta)=\cos\alpha\cos\beta\mp\sin\alpha\sin\beta$$ $$\tan(\alpha\pm\beta)=\frac{\tan\alpha\pm\tan\beta}{1\mp\tan\alpha\tan\beta}$$

Ángulo doble y triple

$$\sin(2\theta)=2\sin\theta\cos\theta$$ $$\cos(2\theta)=\cos^2\theta-\sin^2\theta=1-2\sin^2\theta=2\cos^2\theta-1$$ $$\tan(2\theta)=\frac{2\tan\theta}{1-\tan^2\theta}$$ $$\sin(3\theta)=3\sin\theta-4\sin^3\theta$$ $$\cos(3\theta)=4\cos^3\theta-3\cos\theta$$

Ángulo mitad y reducción de potencias

$$\sin^2\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right)=\frac{1-\cos\theta}{2}$$ $$\cos^2\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right)=\frac{1+\cos\theta}{2}$$ $$\tan\left(\frac{\theta}{2}\right)=\frac{\sin\theta}{1+\cos\theta}=\frac{1-\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}$$ $$\sin^2\theta=\frac{1-\cos(2\theta)}{2},\qquad \cos^2\theta=\frac{1+\cos(2\theta)}{2}$$

Producto a suma

$$\sin\alpha\sin\beta=\frac{1}{2}\left[\cos(\alpha-\beta)-\cos(\alpha+\beta)\right]$$ $$\cos\alpha\cos\beta=\frac{1}{2}\left[\cos(\alpha-\beta)+\cos(\alpha+\beta)\right]$$ $$\sin\alpha\cos\beta=\frac{1}{2}\left[\sin(\alpha+\beta)+\sin(\alpha-\beta)\right]$$

Suma a producto

$$\sin\alpha+\sin\beta=2\sin\left(\frac{\alpha+\beta}{2}\right)\cos\left(\frac{\alpha-\beta}{2}\right)$$ $$\sin\alpha-\sin\beta=2\cos\left(\frac{\alpha+\beta}{2}\right)\sin\left(\frac{\alpha-\beta}{2}\right)$$ $$\cos\alpha+\cos\beta=2\cos\left(\frac{\alpha+\beta}{2}\right)\cos\left(\frac{\alpha-\beta}{2}\right)$$ $$\cos\alpha-\cos\beta=-2\sin\left(\frac{\alpha+\beta}{2}\right)\sin\left(\frac{\alpha-\beta}{2}\right)$$

Cálculo 1 · Límites y derivadas

Propiedades, límites notables y reglas de derivación.

Propiedades de límites

$$\lim_{x\to a}[f(x)\pm g(x)]=L\pm M$$ $$\lim_{x\to a}[c\,f(x)]=cL$$ $$\lim_{x\to a}[f(x)g(x)]=LM$$ $$\lim_{x\to a}\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}=\frac{L}{M},\qquad M\ne0$$ $$\lim_{x\to a}[f(x)]^n=L^n$$ $$\lim_{x\to a}\sqrt[n]{f(x)}=\sqrt[n]{L}$$

Límites notables

$$\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\sin x}{x}=1$$ $$\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\tan x}{x}=1$$ $$\lim_{x\to0}\frac{1-\cos x}{x^2}=\frac12$$ $$\lim_{x\to0}\frac{e^x-1}{x}=1$$ $$\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\ln(1+x)}{x}=1$$ $$\lim_{x\to\infty}\left(1+\frac1x\right)^x=e$$ $$\lim_{x\to0}(1+x)^{1/x}=e$$

Definición de derivada

$$f'(x)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}$$ $$f'(a)=\lim_{x\to a}\frac{f(x)-f(a)}{x-a}$$ $$m_{\text{tangente}}=f'(a)$$ $$y-f(a)=f'(a)(x-a)$$

Reglas algebraicas

$$\frac{d}{dx}(C)=0,\qquad \frac{d}{dx}(x)=1$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}(u^n)=n u^{n-1}u'$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}(u\pm v)=u'\pm v'$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}(uv)=u'v+uv'$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}\left(\frac uv\right)=\frac{u'v-uv'}{v^2}$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}f(g(x))=f'(g(x))g'(x)$$

Exponenciales y logaritmos

$$\frac{d}{dx}(e^u)=e^u u'$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}(a^u)=a^u\ln(a)u'$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}(\ln|u|)=\frac{u'}u$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}(\log_a u)=\frac{u'}{u\ln a}$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}(u^v)=u^v\left(v'\ln u+v\frac{u'}u\right)$$

Funciones trigonométricas

$$\frac{d}{dx}(\sin u)=\cos u\,u'$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}(\cos u)=-\sin u\,u'$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}(\tan u)=\sec^2u\,u'$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}(\cot u)=-\csc^2u\,u'$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}(\sec u)=\sec u\tan u\,u'$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}(\csc u)=-\csc u\cot u\,u'$$

Trigonométricas inversas

$$\frac{d}{dx}(\arcsin u)=\frac{u'}{\sqrt{1-u^2}}$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}(\arccos u)=-\frac{u'}{\sqrt{1-u^2}}$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}(\arctan u)=\frac{u'}{1+u^2}$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}(\operatorname{arccot}u)=-\frac{u'}{1+u^2}$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}(\operatorname{arcsec}u)=\frac{u'}{|u|\sqrt{u^2-1}}$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}(\operatorname{arccsc}u)=-\frac{u'}{|u|\sqrt{u^2-1}}$$

Funciones hiperbólicas

$$\frac{d}{dx}(\sinh u)=\cosh u\,u'$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}(\cosh u)=\sinh u\,u'$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}(\tanh u)=\operatorname{sech}^2u\,u'$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}(\coth u)=-\operatorname{csch}^2u\,u'$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}(\operatorname{sech}u)=-\operatorname{sech}u\tanh u\,u'$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}(\operatorname{csch}u)=-\operatorname{csch}u\coth u\,u'$$

Derivación implícita, paramétrica y aproximación lineal

$$F(x,y)=0\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \frac{dy}{dx}=-\frac{F_x}{F_y}$$ $$x=x(t),\ y=y(t)\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{dy/dt}{dx/dt}$$ $$\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}=\frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{dy}{dx}\right)\Big/\frac{dx}{dt}$$ $$L(x)=f(a)+f'(a)(x-a)$$ $$dy=f'(x)\,dx$$

Cálculo 2 · Integrales

Integrales inmediatas, métodos y aplicaciones fundamentales.

Propiedades básicas

$$\int [f(x)\pm g(x)]\,dx=\int f(x)\,dx\pm\int g(x)\,dx$$ $$\int c f(x)\,dx=c\int f(x)\,dx$$ $$\int u^n\,du=\frac{u^{n+1}}{n+1}+C,\qquad n\ne-1$$ $$\int\frac{du}{u}=\ln|u|+C$$ $$\int e^u\,du=e^u+C$$ $$\int a^u\,du=\frac{a^u}{\ln a}+C$$

Integrales trigonométricas inmediatas

$$\int\sin u\,du=-\cos u+C$$ $$\int\cos u\,du=\sin u+C$$ $$\int\sec^2u\,du=\tan u+C$$ $$\int\csc^2u\,du=-\cot u+C$$ $$\int\sec u\tan u\,du=\sec u+C$$ $$\int\csc u\cot u\,du=-\csc u+C$$

Logarítmicas trigonométricas

$$\int\tan u\,du=\ln|\sec u|+C$$ $$\int\cot u\,du=\ln|\sin u|+C$$ $$\int\sec u\,du=\ln|\sec u+\tan u|+C$$ $$\int\csc u\,du=-\ln|\csc u+\cot u|+C$$

Formas inversas

$$\int\frac{du}{a^2+u^2}=\frac1a\arctan\left(\frac ua\right)+C$$ $$\int\frac{du}{\sqrt{a^2-u^2}}=\arcsin\left(\frac ua\right)+C$$ $$\int\frac{du}{u\sqrt{u^2-a^2}}=\frac1a\operatorname{arcsec}\left(\frac{|u|}{a}\right)+C$$ $$\int\frac{du}{u^2-a^2}=\frac{1}{2a}\ln\left|\frac{u-a}{u+a}\right|+C$$

Integración por sustitución

$$u=g(x),\qquad du=g'(x)\,dx$$ $$\int f(g(x))g'(x)\,dx=\int f(u)\,du$$

Busca una función interna y una expresión proporcional a su derivada.

Integración por partes

$$\int u\,dv=uv-\int v\,du$$

Una guía frecuente para elegir \(u\): logarítmica, inversa trigonométrica, algebraica, trigonométrica y exponencial.

Integración por sustitución trigonométrica

Este método transforma radicales cuadráticos en expresiones trigonométricas. La forma del radical determina la sustitución y el triángulo rectángulo que se utiliza para regresar a la variable original.

Caso 1: \(\sqrt{a^2-u^2}\) \[u=a\sin\theta,\qquad du=a\cos\theta\,d\theta.\] \[\sqrt{a^2-u^2}=a\cos\theta.\]
Caso 2: \(\sqrt{a^2+u^2}\) \[u=a\tan\theta,\qquad du=a\sec^2\theta\,d\theta.\] \[\sqrt{a^2+u^2}=a\sec\theta.\]
Caso 3: \(\sqrt{u^2-a^2}\) \[u=a\sec\theta,\qquad du=a\sec\theta\tan\theta\,d\theta.\] \[\sqrt{u^2-a^2}=a\tan\theta.\]

Identidades que justifican las sustituciones

\[1-\sin^2\theta=\cos^2\theta,\qquad 1+\tan^2\theta=\sec^2\theta,\qquad \sec^2\theta-1=\tan^2\theta.\]

Fracciones parciales

$$\frac{P(x)}{(x-a)(x-b)}=\frac{A}{x-a}+\frac{B}{x-b}$$ $$\frac{P(x)}{(x-a)^n}=\frac{A_1}{x-a}+\frac{A_2}{(x-a)^2}+\cdots+\frac{A_n}{(x-a)^n}$$ $$\frac{P(x)}{x^2+bx+c}=\frac{Ax+B}{x^2+bx+c}$$

Integrales con radicales

$$\int\sqrt{a^2-u^2}\,du=\frac{u}{2}\sqrt{a^2-u^2}+\frac{a^2}{2}\arcsin\left(\frac ua\right)+C$$ $$\int\sqrt{u^2+a^2}\,du=\frac{u}{2}\sqrt{u^2+a^2}+\frac{a^2}{2}\ln\left|u+\sqrt{u^2+a^2}\right|+C$$ $$\int\sqrt{u^2-a^2}\,du=\frac{u}{2}\sqrt{u^2-a^2}-\frac{a^2}{2}\ln\left|u+\sqrt{u^2-a^2}\right|+C$$

Integral definida y teorema fundamental

$$\int_a^b f(x)\,dx=F(b)-F(a),\qquad F'(x)=f(x)$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}\left(\int_a^x f(t)\,dt\right)=f(x)$$ $$\frac{d}{dx}\left(\int_{g(x)}^{h(x)}f(t)\,dt\right)=f(h(x))h'(x)-f(g(x))g'(x)$$ $$A=\int_a^b |f(x)-g(x)|\,dx$$ $$V_{\text{discos}}=\pi\int_a^b\left[R(x)^2-r(x)^2\right]dx$$ $$V_{\text{capas}}=2\pi\int_a^b(\text{radio})(\text{altura})\,dx$$

Cálculo 3 · Ecuaciones diferenciales

Modelos de primer orden, orden superior, sistemas y transformada de Laplace.

Variables separables

$$\frac{dy}{dx}=g(x)h(y)$$ $$\frac{dy}{h(y)}=g(x)\,dx$$ $$\int\frac{dy}{h(y)}=\int g(x)\,dx+C$$

Ecuación lineal de primer orden

$$y'+P(x)y=Q(x)$$ $$\mu(x)=e^{\int P(x)\,dx}$$ $$y=\frac{1}{\mu(x)}\left[\int \mu(x)Q(x)\,dx+C\right]$$

Ecuación de Bernoulli

$$y'+P(x)y=Q(x)y^n$$ $$v=y^{1-n}$$ $$v'+(1-n)P(x)v=(1-n)Q(x)$$

Ecuaciones exactas

$$M(x,y)\,dx+N(x,y)\,dy=0$$ $$\frac{\partial M}{\partial y}=\frac{\partial N}{\partial x}$$ $$\psi_x=M,\qquad \psi_y=N,\qquad \psi(x,y)=C$$

Homogéneas de primer orden

$$\frac{dy}{dx}=F\left(\frac yx\right)$$ $$v=\frac yx,\qquad y=vx,\qquad y'=v+xv'$$

Crecimiento, decaimiento y logística

$$\frac{dP}{dt}=kP\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad P(t)=P_0e^{kt}$$ $$\frac{dP}{dt}=rP\left(1-\frac PK\right)$$ $$P(t)=\frac{K}{1+Ae^{-rt}},\qquad A=\frac{K-P_0}{P_0}$$

Ecuaciones lineales homogéneas de orden superior

$$a_ny^{(n)}+a_{n-1}y^{(n-1)}+\cdots+a_1y'+a_0y=0$$ $$a_nr^n+a_{n-1}r^{n-1}+\cdots+a_1r+a_0=0$$

Raíces reales distintas

$$y=C_1e^{r_1x}+C_2e^{r_2x}+\cdots+C_ne^{r_nx}$$

Raíz real repetida \(r\) de multiplicidad \(m\)

$$y=e^{rx}\left(C_1+C_2x+\cdots+C_mx^{m-1}\right)$$

Raíces complejas \(r=\alpha\pm\beta i\)

$$y=e^{\alpha x}\left(C_1\cos\beta x+C_2\sin\beta x\right)$$

No homogéneas

$$a y''+b y'+cy=g(x)$$ $$y=y_h+y_p$$

Para coeficientes indeterminados, la forma de \(y_p\) se elige según \(g(x)\). Si se repite con \(y_h\), se multiplica por una potencia adecuada de \(x\).

Variación de parámetros

$$y_p=-y_1\int\frac{y_2g}{W}\,dx+y_2\int\frac{y_1g}{W}\,dx$$ $$W=y_1y_2'-y_1'y_2$$

Sistemas lineales

$$\mathbf{x}'=A\mathbf{x}$$ $$\mathbf{x}(t)=e^{At}\mathbf{x}(0)$$ $$\det(A-\lambda I)=0$$ $$\mathbf{x}(t)=\sum_i C_i\mathbf{v}_i e^{\lambda_i t}$$

Transformada de Laplace

$$\mathcal{L}\{f(t)\}=\int_0^\infty e^{-st}f(t)\,dt$$ $$\mathcal{L}\{f'\}=sF(s)-f(0)$$ $$\mathcal{L}\{f''\}=s^2F(s)-sf(0)-f'(0)$$ $$\mathcal{L}\{e^{at}f(t)\}=F(s-a)$$

Transformadas frecuentes

$$\mathcal{L}\{1\}=\frac1s,\qquad \mathcal{L}\{t^n\}=\frac{n!}{s^{n+1}}$$ $$\mathcal{L}\{e^{at}\}=\frac{1}{s-a}$$ $$\mathcal{L}\{\sin bt\}=\frac{b}{s^2+b^2},\qquad \mathcal{L}\{\cos bt\}=\frac{s}{s^2+b^2}$$ $$\mathcal{L}\{\sinh bt\}=\frac{b}{s^2-b^2},\qquad \mathcal{L}\{\cosh bt\}=\frac{s}{s^2-b^2}$$ $$\mathcal{L}\{f*g\}=F(s)G(s)$$

Cálculo 4 · Fourier y expansión de Taylor

Series de potencias, aproximación local y representación frecuencial.

Polinomio y serie de Taylor

$$P_n(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\frac{f^{(k)}(a)}{k!}(x-a)^k$$ $$f(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{f^{(k)}(a)}{k!}(x-a)^k$$ $$R_n(x)=\frac{f^{(n+1)}(\xi)}{(n+1)!}(x-a)^{n+1}$$

Cuando \(a=0\), la expansión se denomina serie de Maclaurin.

Series de Maclaurin comunes

$$e^x=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^n}{n!}$$ $$\sin x=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1)^n\frac{x^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)!}$$ $$\cos x=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}(-1)^n\frac{x^{2n}}{(2n)!}$$ $$\frac{1}{1-x}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^n,\qquad |x|<1$$

Más expansiones frecuentes

$$\ln(1+x)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{n+1}\frac{x^n}{n},\qquad -1

Serie de Fourier con periodo \(T\)

$$f(t)=\frac{a_0}{2}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left[a_n\cos(n\omega_0t)+b_n\sin(n\omega_0t)\right]$$ $$\omega_0=\frac{2\pi}{T}$$ $$a_0=\frac{2}{T}\int_{t_0}^{t_0+T}f(t)\,dt$$ $$a_n=\frac{2}{T}\int_{t_0}^{t_0+T}f(t)\cos(n\omega_0t)\,dt$$ $$b_n=\frac{2}{T}\int_{t_0}^{t_0+T}f(t)\sin(n\omega_0t)\,dt$$

Forma en intervalo \([-L,L]\)

$$f(x)=\frac{a_0}{2}+\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left[a_n\cos\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right)+b_n\sin\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right)\right]$$ $$a_n=\frac1L\int_{-L}^{L}f(x)\cos\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right)dx$$ $$b_n=\frac1L\int_{-L}^{L}f(x)\sin\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right)dx$$

Simetrías

$$f(-x)=f(x)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad b_n=0$$ $$a_n=\frac{2}{L}\int_0^L f(x)\cos\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right)dx$$ $$f(-x)=-f(x)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad a_0=a_n=0$$ $$b_n=\frac{2}{L}\int_0^L f(x)\sin\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right)dx$$

Forma compleja de Fourier

$$f(t)=\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}c_ne^{in\omega_0t}$$ $$c_n=\frac1T\int_{t_0}^{t_0+T}f(t)e^{-in\omega_0t}\,dt$$ $$c_0=\frac{a_0}{2},\qquad c_n=\frac{a_n-ib_n}{2}$$

Transformada de Fourier

$$F(\omega)=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(t)e^{-i\omega t}\,dt$$ $$f(t)=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}F(\omega)e^{i\omega t}\,d\omega$$ $$\mathcal{F}\{f'(t)\}=i\omega F(\omega)$$ $$\mathcal{F}\{f*g\}=F(\omega)G(\omega)$$

Identidad de Parseval

$$\frac1T\int_{t_0}^{t_0+T}|f(t)|^2dt=\frac{a_0^2}{4}+\frac12\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(a_n^2+b_n^2)$$ $$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}|f(t)|^2dt=\frac{1}{2\pi}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}|F(\omega)|^2d\omega$$

Física · Compendio principal

Mecánica, fluidos, termodinámica, electricidad, magnetismo, ondas y óptica.

Vectores

$$\vec A=A_x\hat i+A_y\hat j+A_z\hat k$$ $$|\vec A|=\sqrt{A_x^2+A_y^2+A_z^2}$$ $$\hat u_A=\frac{\vec A}{|\vec A|}$$ $$\vec A\cdot\vec B=AB\cos\theta$$ $$|\vec A\times\vec B|=AB\sin\theta$$

Cinemática rectilínea

$$v_{\text{med}}=\frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t},\qquad a_{\text{med}}=\frac{\Delta v}{\Delta t}$$ $$v=\frac{dx}{dt},\qquad a=\frac{dv}{dt}=\frac{d^2x}{dt^2}$$ $$x=x_0+vt\qquad(\text{MRU})$$ $$v=v_0+at$$ $$x=x_0+v_0t+\frac12at^2$$ $$v^2=v_0^2+2a(x-x_0)$$

Movimiento parabólico

$$v_{0x}=v_0\cos\theta,\qquad v_{0y}=v_0\sin\theta$$ $$x=x_0+v_0\cos\theta\,t$$ $$y=y_0+v_0\sin\theta\,t-\frac12gt^2$$ $$v_y=v_0\sin\theta-gt$$ $$T=\frac{2v_0\sin\theta}{g}$$ $$R=\frac{v_0^2\sin(2\theta)}{g}$$ $$h_{\max}=\frac{v_0^2\sin^2\theta}{2g}$$

Movimiento circular

$$\omega=\frac{d\theta}{dt},\qquad \alpha=\frac{d\omega}{dt}$$ $$\omega=\omega_0+\alpha t$$ $$\theta=\theta_0+\omega_0t+\frac12\alpha t^2$$ $$v=\omega r,\qquad a_t=\alpha r$$ $$a_c=\frac{v^2}{r}=\omega^2r$$ $$\omega=2\pi f,\qquad T=\frac1f$$

Dinámica

$$\sum\vec F=m\vec a$$ $$\vec W=m\vec g$$ $$F_s\le\mu_sN,\qquad F_k=\mu_kN$$ $$F_c=\frac{mv^2}{r}$$ $$\vec F_{AB}=-\vec F_{BA}$$

Trabajo, energía y potencia

$$W=\int\vec F\cdot d\vec r$$ $$W=Fd\cos\theta$$ $$K=\frac12mv^2$$ $$U_g=mgh,\qquad U_e=\frac12kx^2$$ $$W_{\text{neto}}=\Delta K$$ $$E_i+W_{nc}=E_f$$ $$P=\frac{dW}{dt}=\vec F\cdot\vec v$$

Cantidad de movimiento e impulso

$$\vec p=m\vec v$$ $$\vec J=\int\vec F\,dt=\Delta\vec p$$ $$\sum\vec p_i=\sum\vec p_f$$ $$e=\frac{v_{2f}-v_{1f}}{v_{1i}-v_{2i}}$$ $$\vec r_{cm}=\frac{\sum m_i\vec r_i}{\sum m_i}$$

Rotación y equilibrio

$$\vec\tau=\vec r\times\vec F$$ $$\sum\tau=I\alpha$$ $$K_{rot}=\frac12I\omega^2$$ $$\vec L=I\vec\omega$$ $$\sum\vec F=0,\qquad \sum\tau=0$$ $$I=\int r^2\,dm$$

Gravitación

$$F=G\frac{m_1m_2}{r^2}$$ $$g=G\frac{M}{r^2}$$ $$U=-G\frac{Mm}{r}$$ $$v_{orb}=\sqrt{\frac{GM}{r}}$$ $$v_{esc}=\sqrt{\frac{2GM}{R}}$$ $$T^2=\frac{4\pi^2}{GM}r^3$$

Fluidos

$$\rho=\frac{m}{V},\qquad P=\frac{F}{A}$$ $$P=P_0+\rho gh$$ $$F_B=\rho_{fluido}gV_{desplazado}$$ $$A_1v_1=A_2v_2$$ $$P+\frac12\rho v^2+\rho gh=\text{constante}$$ $$Q=Av$$

Termodinámica

$$Q=mc\Delta T$$ $$Q=mL$$ $$PV=nRT$$ $$\Delta U=Q-W$$ $$W=\int_{V_i}^{V_f}P\,dV$$ $$\eta=\frac{W}{Q_H}=1-\frac{Q_C}{Q_H}$$ $$\eta_{Carnot}=1-\frac{T_C}{T_H}$$

Electrostática

$$F=k\frac{|q_1q_2|}{r^2}$$ $$\vec E=\frac{\vec F}{q_0}=k\frac{q}{r^2}\hat r$$ $$V=k\frac qr$$ $$U=qV=k\frac{q_1q_2}{r}$$ $$\Phi_E=\int\vec E\cdot d\vec A$$ $$\oint\vec E\cdot d\vec A=\frac{Q_{enc}}{\varepsilon_0}$$

Capacitancia y circuitos

$$C=\frac QV$$ $$C_{placas}=\varepsilon\frac Ad$$ $$U_C=\frac12CV^2=\frac{Q^2}{2C}$$ $$V=IR$$ $$P=VI=I^2R=\frac{V^2}{R}$$ $$R=\rho\frac LA$$ $$\sum I_{entra}=\sum I_{sale},\qquad \sum\Delta V=0$$

Resistencias y capacitores equivalentes

$$R_s=R_1+R_2+\cdots$$ $$\frac1{R_p}=\frac1{R_1}+\frac1{R_2}+\cdots$$ $$C_p=C_1+C_2+\cdots$$ $$\frac1{C_s}=\frac1{C_1}+\frac1{C_2}+\cdots$$ $$q_{carga}=CV\left(1-e^{-t/RC}\right)$$ $$q_{descarga}=Q_0e^{-t/RC}$$

Magnetismo e inducción

Ondas y sonido

$$v=\lambda f$$ $$y(x,t)=A\sin(kx-\omega t+\phi)$$ $$k=\frac{2\pi}{\lambda},\qquad \omega=2\pi f$$ $$v_{cuerda}=\sqrt{\frac{T}{\mu}}$$ $$I=\frac PA,\qquad \beta=10\log_{10}\left(\frac I{I_0}\right)$$ $$f'=f\frac{v\pm v_o}{v\mp v_s}$$

Óptica geométrica

$$n=\frac cv$$ $$n_1\sin\theta_1=n_2\sin\theta_2$$ $$\theta_i=\theta_r$$ $$\frac1f=\frac1{d_o}+\frac1{d_i}$$ $$m=\frac{h_i}{h_o}=-\frac{d_i}{d_o}$$ $$P_{lente}=\frac1f\quad(f\text{ en metros})$$
